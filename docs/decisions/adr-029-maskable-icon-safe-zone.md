---
title: The maskable icon is framed against the safe zone, not the canvas
date: 2026-07-25
---

## Context
The app mark is drawn once and shipped three ways: the tile inside the app, the manifest icon with `purpose: "any"`, and the manifest icon with `purpose: "maskable"`. All three had the mark at 0.625 of their canvas, on the reasoning that one scale everywhere means one mark everywhere.

That reasoning is wrong for the maskable one, and it showed: installed on a phone, the icon read visibly bigger than the same mark inside the app, filling about four fifths of the tile against 0.625 in the app.

A launcher does not show the maskable canvas as drawn. It cuts its own shape out of it and only the central 80% is guaranteed to survive. A mark at 0.625 of the full canvas is therefore 0.78 of what is actually shown, with its extremes 0.39 of the canvas out from the centre against a 0.40 safe radius — inside the circle by a hair, and with nothing left over for a launcher that masks more tightly.

## Decision
Frame the maskable variant against the safe zone rather than the canvas: the mark sits at **0.5** of the canvas, which is 0.625 of the 80% that is shown. The `any` variant keeps 0.625 of its canvas, because that canvas is shown as drawn.

The two variants also differ in their background: the maskable one bleeds to every edge with no corners of its own, since the corners are the launcher's to draw.

Both are generated by `icons/build.sh` and committed, and the safe-zone rule is asserted by a unit test — the crop happens on the phone, so looking at the file cannot catch a regression. The `any` icon is rendered from the shipped `public/favicon.svg` rather than a copy of it, so only the maskable variant needs a source of its own.

## Why
"One scale everywhere" was the right instinct applied to the wrong measurement. What has to match between the three drawings is how big the mark looks, and for the maskable icon that is measured against the visible tile, not the file. Scaling by the safe-zone fraction is what makes the three actually identical instead of nominally identical.

## Consequences
The `apple-touch-icon` now points at the maskable file too. iOS draws its own rounded corners and does not honour transparency, so the `any` tile's transparent corners came out black; the full-bleed variant has none. It costs a slightly smaller mark on iOS, which does not crop, and that is the better of the two errors.

The icon PNGs are committed and the generator is a development tool — nothing in CI or in the Docker image runs it, and it needs `rsvg-convert` locally. See [[development]].

The sources cannot simply live beside their outputs in `public/`: the PWA config precaches `**/*.svg`, so a source there would be downloaded by every user. `icons/` holds the one source that is not itself shipped.
