## What wordavi does

wordavi helps you get fast and confident with numbers in Spanish — the kind you actually need at a till, on a price tag, or weighing produce. It's not a general Spanish course; it's focused practice for one skill: hearing or reading a number and knowing what it means, and going the other way too.

Once it's live, you'll be able to practice in a few different ways:

- **Number to Spanish** — you're shown a number and write out how to say it.
- **Spanish to number** — you're shown Spanish text and pick or type the number it represents.
- **Listening practice** — a number is read aloud in Spanish and you write down what you heard.
- **Multiple choice** — quick-fire recognition when you just want to move fast.
- **Speak it back** — an optional mode where you say the number out loud and wordavi checks it, on devices that support voice input. If your device or browser doesn't support it, this mode just stays out of the way.
- **Prices and quantities** — the practical stuff: euro prices like "cuatro con setenta y cinco", and grocery amounts like "dos kilos y medio" or "250 gramos".

You control the difficulty with a range slider — anywhere from single digits up to a million — and a toggle for whether decimals are included. The app interface can be shown in Russian or English, but the numbers you're learning are always Spanish (es-ES).

As you practice, wordavi keeps light track of your streaks, a daily goal, and your score, and it quietly resurfaces the kinds of numbers you tend to get wrong.
